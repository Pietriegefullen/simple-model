

Michaelis-Menten
================

with S << Km => 1st order kinetics
-> v grows approximately linear with S.

with S >> Km => 0th order kinetics
-> v is approximately independent of S (saturated)

small Km values indicate high affinity to the substrate, 
=> small amounts suffice to reach half velocity



Hydrogenotrophic Methanogenesis
===============================

CUE is very low: 1% to 5%, i.e. 0.01 - 0.05
K_m for H2 is low: (high affinity), around 1 to 6 micromoles
K_m for CO2 is much higher: 400 to 1200 micromoles



Fe3
===

Geobacter is highly affine to Acetate Km for acetate around 10 - 50 micromole
Mischkinetik: measured: 0.8 micromole (up to 1000 micromole)
